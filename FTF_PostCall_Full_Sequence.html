For AJ — What This Is
This is the follow-up sequence that fires after the Sunday call — whether a producer attended, missed it, or you called them personally.

The goal: Move them from "interested" to "building their store" over 21 days. The sequence ends automatically the moment they start their store setup — so there's no awkward overlap with onboarding.

Each touch is shown in two versions:
  • Personal — written in your voice, from you directly
  • Platform — written as From The Farm the brand
Every touch has two CTAs: Start Your Store for producers who are ready, and Schedule a Call with You for producers who still have questions. Both exits remove them from the sequence automatically.

One thing we need from you before this goes live:
  • The URL where producers start their store setup
  • Your calendar link for booking calls
  • Confirmation that you can save + share the Sunday call recording each week (used in the no-show path)
  • Your preference: Personal voice or Platform voice? Pick one — we'll build that version.
